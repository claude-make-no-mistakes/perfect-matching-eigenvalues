# Independent matching certificates

Verify every core without singleton parts of weight 2 through 16 at its first eligible layer, with exactly two singleton parts. For each relation compare its target eigenvalue with every nontrivial competing eigenspace. Equality is allowed. Keep the signed eigenvalues. The infinite extension depends separately on the edge-conditioning proof in the matching report.

The independent checker uses integer Gram–Schmidt at Jack parameter two in the power-sum basis. Its inputs are only integer partitions. The labelled monomial expansion in the implementation sums over injections of distinct variable indices. Subtracting individual collisions after multiplication by a new power sum gives its recurrence. Ascending lexicographic order extends dominance, so orthogonalizing these monomials gives the Jack basis up to scaling. Dividing each row by its identity coefficient fixes its scale. The power-sum expansion then gives the matching eigenmatrix by Theorem 2.2.1 of [Lindzey, Matchings and Representation Theory](https://dspacemainprd01.lib.uwaterloo.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/9d33772f-c7dc-409f-a4ed-521da8d87890/content).

Before generating the new tables, the checker passed the known full degree-three spectrum, the degree-four exception, identity ties, weighted trace identities and explicit edge-indicator eigenvalues. The SHA-256 of the checker at that checkpoint was `229872eaa7d33df45d84a8f57293612776781ff4497edfda3e6469b9c130a57f`. Each table records the largest competing eigenvalue, its gap to the target, and the number of eigenspaces considered before removing the trivial and target eigenspaces.
