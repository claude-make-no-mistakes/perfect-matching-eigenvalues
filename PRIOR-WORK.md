# Related work

This repository builds on and addresses open conjectures from:

- **Gupta, Herman, Lacaze-Masmonteil, Maleki, and Meagher**, *Eigenvalues of the perfect matching association scheme*, [arXiv:2510.17135v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.17135v2) (2025); published in *Electron. J. Combin.*, [doi:10.37236/15092](https://doi.org/10.37236/15092). They prove the signed second-eigenvalue ordering for several explicit families (including one-singleton hooks $(n-1,1)$ for $n \ge 6$ in Corollary 3.5) and for sufficiently large first part, formulate the general second-eigenspace question (Conjecture 1.3), the minimum unnormalized spectral gap question (Conjecture 6.1), and the diameter bound $n - 1$ (Conjecture 6.2), and provide exact spectral tables through $n = 15$ in their companion [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Himanshugupta23/Perfect-Matching-Association-Scheme).
- **M. K. Srinivasan**, *The perfect matching association scheme*, *Algebraic Combinatorics* **3** (2020), no. 3, 559–591, [doi:10.5802/alco.104](https://doi.org/10.5802/alco.104). Gives the multiplicity-free decomposition $\mathbb{R}[\Omega_n] \cong \bigoplus_{\lambda \vdash n} V_{2\lambda}$ and recursive eigenvalue computations.
- **N. Lindzey**, *Matchings and Representation Theory*, Ph.D. thesis, University of Waterloo (2018), Chapter 2, Theorem 2.2.1 ([PDF](https://dspacemainprd01.lib.uwaterloo.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/9d33772f-c7dc-409f-a4ed-521da8d87890/content)). Relates the zonal (Jack $\alpha = 2$) polynomial expansion in the power-sum basis to the eigenvalues of the perfect matching association scheme.
- **A. Herman**, CMS Winter 2026 abstract ([CMS Winter 2026](https://www2.cms.math.ca/Events/winter26/abs/asa)), announcing progress on the second-eigenspace question.

Our single-cycle starting cases ($s \ge 5$) use Corollary 3.5 of Gupta et al., and our minimum-gap proof uses the switch spectral gap $\Delta_\sigma = 2n - 1$ (also derived directly from the central transposition sum $nI + 2A_\sigma$).
